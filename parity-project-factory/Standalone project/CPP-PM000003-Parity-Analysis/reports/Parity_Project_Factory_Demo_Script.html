09

LLM scope, security and billing

This is the most important architecture-and-risk moment.

3 min
"The model receives no direct database access. It receives only eligible, redacted candidate records under candidate, batch and projected-spend limits; its findings return through validation."

Say

Direct store, offering and deal values are transformed with a salted SHA-256-derived token. A secret salt from a vault prevents dictionary-style reversal. This is pseudonymization, not anonymization, because prices, dates, direction and business context remain.

The latest 50-candidate run estimated 7,366 input and 7,500 output tokens for $0.093415 at illustrative prices. No provider invoice exists and the measured in-pipeline model cost is $0.00.

Show and prove

  • config/llm_guardrails.json: 500 candidates, 50 rows, 20 batches, $5 per run.
  • output/llm_candidates.json: h_ identifiers.
  • output/cost/llm_cost_estimate.json: estimate_not_billed.
  • Explain: token cost equals input, cached input and output rates divided by one million.
Security statement: "The demo has require_salt: false for usability. Production must require a vault-injected salt, approve provider data terms, and add DLP review."
Scale statement: "At 6M records and a 20% candidate rate, the illustrative LLM bill is $2,241.96. The 20% rate comes from the demo and must be validated on production data."
10

Human review and retry control

Demonstrate safe continuation without pretending approval happened.

1.5 min
"Review has the highest decision precedence, but the operational workflow is resilient: an absent review becomes an audit obligation, not an indefinite system stop."

Say

Manual review can override an eligible classification. An LLM finding marked INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE leaves the deterministic verdict unchanged and records HUMAN_APPROVAL_REQUIRED. Each task may retry twice. Exhaustion records HUMAN_INSPECTION_REQUIRED and automatic resume remains possible.

Show and prove

  • Open the review columns in the workbook.
  • Show reviewer identity, timestamp, notes and reviewed classification fields.
  • Show the task state and retry evidence.
  • Emphasize that markers are traceable and remain visible in output.
Audience takeaway: resilience does not erase accountability; it changes a missing human action into an explicit auditable state.
10A

Bounded self-healing and recovery

Show automatic resilience without presenting unsafe autonomous repair.

2 min
"Self-healing here means controlled recovery from a transient task failure: repeat the same safe operation, preserve evidence, then resume from the first unresolved stage. It does not mean the system silently invents a mapping, changes a rule, or performs a destructive repair."

Explain the implemented mechanism

  1. The orchestrator reads state/task-state.json and finds the requested or first unresolved task.
  2. It marks the task RUNNING and executes the mapped specialist command.
  3. A transient failure can be retried twice, producing up to three total attempts with the same bounded command.
  4. Every attempt records the command, start time, duration, return code, and shortened output.
  5. Success changes the task to SUCCESS. Exhaustion changes it to HUMAN_INSPECTION_REQUIRED.
  6. After the cause is corrected or approved, the orchestrator can resume from that unresolved task without rerunning successful upstream work.

Show and prove

  • Open state/task-state.json and point to task status, attempts, maximum retries and evidence.
  • Show the configured policy: two retries, explicit resume, and recorded human inspection on exhaustion.
  • Use the prompt: “Resume pipeline” for the first unresolved task.
  • Or use: “Resume failed ims-migrate task” for a named failure.
  • Point out that BLOCKED is different from HUMAN_INSPECTION_REQUIRED: a prerequisite is missing versus an executed task exhausted its attempts.
Self-healing boundary: retries and resume are automatic; diagnosis and corrective change are governed. Missing day maps, corrupt data, ambiguous requirements, schema incompatibility, production credentials, destructive refresh and replacement require explicit evidence or approval.
Audience takeaway: the pipeline recovers from transient execution failures without losing state, while unsafe or semantic failures remain visible and are escalated rather than hidden.
Transition: “That bounded resilience protects the workflow. The final section shows the measurable business case and the controls required before production.”
11

Business assurance, ROI and the ask

Close on the decision: controlled pilot, measurable value.

2.5 min
"The 6-million-record planning case is economically attractive at illustrative rates, but the approval request is for a controlled pilot that validates the assumptions, not for an unbounded production rollout."

Say

At 6M records, 20% eligibility means 1.2M candidates. At 70% acceptance, 840,000 outcomes retain only two minutes of residual review instead of eight. Under the displayed $45/hour labor and $0.001868/candidate assumptions, the model produces approximately $3.78M/month and $45.33M/year of net capacity value.

The labor result is not automatically cash. It becomes ROI only when staffing, outsourcing, throughput or service capacity changes are verified.

Show the decision gates

  • Contract-backed model and currency rates.
  • Provider retention, residency, encryption and training-use terms.
  • Vault salt and DLP/privacy review.
  • Central daily/monthly budget, concurrency and kill switch.
  • Provider receipt reconciliation.
  • Quality, rework and reviewer-time measurement.
Recommended ask: approve a production pilot with a fixed volume, fixed budget, named reviewers, named data owner and measurable success criteria.
QA

Expected questions and concise answers

Keep the close factual and confident.

As needed
Does the LLM change the database?

No. The Python pipeline validates JSON findings before any supported field update; the model has no direct database access.

Is the LLM cost $0.00?

The in-pipeline model cost is $0.00 because the pipeline makes no model API call. The estimated external hand-off cost is separate.

Does the salt make data anonymous?

No. It strengthens pseudonymization of selected identifiers. Dates, prices, row IDs and business context still require privacy review.

Can 6M records be processed now?

The estimate is a planning model. A production benchmark is required for throughput, concurrency, failure recovery and platform cost.

Does the $5 ceiling cap the monthly bill?

No. It caps one run. An aggregate monthly budget and kill switch are production requirements.

What proves the economics?

Only a pilot that compares before/after handling time, acceptance quality, rework, provider receipts and realized staffing or capacity outcomes.

Why use specialized agents?

They narrow context, encode prerequisites and make ownership visible. Deterministic scripts remain the executable core.

What makes this reproducible?

A confirmed spec, generated configuration, vendored core, version metadata, idempotent behavior, tests, validation and persisted artifacts.
